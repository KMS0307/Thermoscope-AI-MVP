# 1. WHAT WE ARE BUILDING

Build an AI-powered geospatial intelligence platform that:

1. Receives satellite thermal anomaly observations from NASA FIRMS.
2. Cleans and validates the observations.
3. Uses OpenStreetMap data to understand what exists around each anomaly.
4. Extracts thermal, temporal and geospatial features.
5. Classifies the anomaly into a likely category.
6. Detects whether the thermal source is persistent.
7. Calculates a transparent 0–100 risk score.
8. Generates alerts for high-risk incidents.
9. Displays everything on an interactive map/dashboard.
10. Explains WHY an anomaly received its classification and risk score.

The central concept is:

NASA FIRMS
↓
Thermal Anomaly
↓
OSM Context
↓
Feature Engineering
↓
ML Classification
↓
Spatio-Temporal Persistence
↓
Risk Scoring
↓
Alert
↓
Interactive Dashboard

# 12. RISK ENGINE

Create a transparent risk score from 0–100.

Use:

```text
Thermal Intensity       25%
FIRMS Confidence        15%
Persistence             25%
Industrial Proximity    15%
Population Proximity   10%
Cluster Density          5%
Night Activity           5%
```

Formula:

```text
risk =
0.25 * thermal_intensity
+ 0.15 * firms_confidence
+ 0.25 * persistence
+ 0.15 * industrial_proximity
+ 0.10 * population_proximity
+ 0.05 * cluster_density
+ 0.05 * night_activity
```

Normalize to 0–100.

Risk:

```text
0–24     LOW
25–49    MODERATE
50–74    HIGH
75–100   CRITICAL
```

Show the complete breakdown in the UI.
